This SVG diagram illustrates the **seven cardinal movements of labor** in a clear, educational format:

1. **Engagement** — The fetal head enters the pelvic inlet (biparietal diameter passes through)
2. **Descent** — Downward movement through the birth canal driven by contractions
3. **Flexion** — Chin tucks to chest, presenting the smallest diameter (suboccipitobregmatic)
4. **Internal Rotation** — Occiput rotates from transverse to anterior beneath the pubis
5. **Extension** — Head extends around the pubic arch as the face sweeps over the perineum
6. **External Rotation (Restitution)** — Head rotates back 45° to realign with the shoulders
7. **Expulsion** — Shoulders and body are delivered

Each panel shows a **sagittal cross-section** of the maternal pelvis with the fetal head at the appropriate position and orientation, along with directional arrows indicating the key movement. The color-coded headers and numbered badges help track the sequential progression.
